## Changes made for the study

The study digitized and structured lexical material as database rows, curated
words/meanings with language collaborators, retained category and stable entry
IDs, and applied documented retrieval normalization. Benchmark queries include
synthetic exact/prefix/edit perturbations; these are research transformations,
not errors attributed to the original book. Lexical anchors and outcomes are
source-recovery evidence, not exhaustive relevance judgments.

The later approved snapshot contains 4,587 entries. Relative to the historical
4,584-entry snapshot it includes three additional approved records with IDs
4585, 4599 and 4601. The historical entries retain their fingerprinted lexical
fields. This snapshot relationship is disclosed; no claim is made that every
later project record was individually matched to a printed book entry.

The exported fixture removes account/authentication and individual contribution
history. Its retained fields and fingerprint are described in
`data/lexical/FIXTURE_PROVENANCE.json`. The original PDF is not included.

rights in every software file or every person's contribution. This update does
not choose a new software license. Code ownership and software-release terms
remain separate from the source-derived lexical material's published license.
